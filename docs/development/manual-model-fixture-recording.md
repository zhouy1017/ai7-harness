# Converting development captures to model fixtures

The former per-recording attendance and permission procedure has been removed under [ADR 0089](../adr/0089-adopt-orca-dispatch-and-value-first-live-model-delivery.md). Git history retains the old text.

Necessary development validation follows the [development credential and evidence boundary](../adr/0067-authorize-the-opencode-go-development-credential-with-live-once-testing.md) and ADR 0089: record the new question, reuse existing evidence when it answers that question, and capture any necessary new result through the product's admitted path and protected Ledger/Result Cache. A developer-live launch names its question as `--validation-case <family>/<case>`, and names any substantive delta as `--validation-delta input:<rationale>` or `retry-unknown:<rationale>`. The ledger refuses, without sending, a call that existing evidence already answers ([source checkout](source-checkout.md), #808). [#387](https://github.com/zhouy1017/ai7-harness/issues/387) owns the first model-derived analysis fixture.

Provider Processing v2 remains an immutable runtime record. Its retained bytes are not an alternative development dispatch procedure and do not establish that a new recording has been authorized or executed. Policy migration happens in the implementation slice, not by editing that record.

Review and sanitize fixture candidates with source/model/contract provenance before repository admission. Raw Provider payloads and secrets remain outside repositories, Issues, logs and hosted CI; admitted fixtures are development evidence, never fresh business answers or current-model quality proof.

Run the existing converter on the developer host, naming an admitted item or full request digest in the protected external cache:

```text
node tools/generate-model-fixture.mjs --from-cache <item-or-digest> --cache-root <external-cache-root> --identity <fixture-id> --description <safe-description> --out <external-staging-file>
```

All five arguments are required. Output under any `tests/fixtures/` directory is refused; a sanitized candidate enters the repository only through its separately scoped review. The tool prints the output path or a bounded refusal, never the request or response. This conversion needs no Provider call or credential.

For exact sample1's exemption under [ADR 0079 §5](../adr/0079-record-the-owner-s-decisions-of-2026-09-10-on-storage-policies-export-egress-providers-and-samplebooks.md), add the paired `--sample1-store <external-ai7.sqlite>` and `--sample1-task <captured-task-intent-id>` arguments. After normal bootstrap, the generator bundles the existing source extraction, manifest and message owners in memory. It verifies this checkout's exact admitted native carrier hash, reconstructs the entire captured manifest from its extracted blocks, checks the frozen task records' hashes and model/contract binding, then rebuilds the selected unit, reduction, sampling or reflection message from those records. A path, filename, asserted hash or validation label alone cannot grant the exemption. The external store opens read-only; a mismatch refuses `FIXTURE_GEN/sample1-source-unproved` without emitting content. Verified sample1 free text stays intact, including quoted blocks and invalid result shapes; citation identities still become mapped placeholders. Other sources retain the echo bound and receive no repository admission from conversion.

Issue #387's admitted `sample1-recorded-v41-baseline` combines the reviewed candidates for `S40/first-baseline/9..20`, captured on 2026-10-12 through `opencode-go/deepseek-v4.1-flash`. Every free-text field and mapping was reviewed against exact sample1 before admission. It preserves eight units (unit 1's seven unresolved strings remain schema-invalid), one reduction, two sampling turns and one reflection: the original completed-with-gaps 7/8 outcome. Source-recorded usage is 38,287 input and 113,041 output tokens over twelve calls; conversion and regression replay incur zero new Provider transmissions. Earlier V4 items are not in this fixture. Replaying those records proves failure handling and durable business behavior, not a new model call or editorial-quality validation.

Issue #826's admitted `sample1-recorded-v41-range` holds six reviewed real captures of the range recomputation of units 1 and 2 under baseline prompt contract /2 (`89776b1b…`), captured on 2026-10-13 through the same route and model:

- units 1 and 2 and the cross-unit reduction, `S40/reanalyze-range/2..4`, proved against their first-repair Task;
- the three sampling turns under sampling contract /2 (`1bfd29ec…`), `S40/reanalyze-range/12..14`, proved against the second validation Task.

Its source-recorded usage is that of those six calls. It has no reflection: the real reflection was asked with an accounting in which the replayed units and reduction incurred no usage, which this fixture replay does not reproduce. J-04 explicitly asserts the resulting reflection fixture-mismatch gap. The recorded /1 entries of `sample1-recorded-v41-baseline` are never re-keyed or relabeled as /2.

The exact-sample1 proof accepts the task contract the captured Task actually bound — the current /2, or the retained /1 of the original Run — and rebuilds a sampling message under that selection's sampling contract. A sampling capture's stage contract is read from its exact /1 or /2 instruction; missing, altered or unknown instructions are refused. The capture's recorded stage contract must agree. Only that validated public instruction segment is excluded from manuscript-echo scanning; source blocks, findings and other carried turns remain checked.

Conversion recognizes the existing unit, reduction, sampling, reflection, factual/review/evaluation/writing and dialogue contracts already consumed by the deterministic adapter. Recognition does not admit a live Task or establish model capability. Each entry keeps structured `capture` provenance: original and selected item, actual model, actual stage `promptContractDigest` and enclosing `taskPromptContractDigest`, capture time, exact request/response hashes, namespace and generation, validation purpose, explicit block/finding placeholder mappings and the sanitized response hash. The adapter refuses a namespaced converted entry from a different enclosing task contract. Recorded usage remains the original capture's usage; fixture replay incurs no Provider transmission.

Namespaced entries must agree with their ledger's route, endpoint, request shape, model, full request digest, source item, stage and task contracts, generation, pre-send evidence position and recorded usage; a capture a later capture of the same exact request moved aside is read from its `superseded` file. An item invalidation retires its capture. A validation-case invalidation retires every capture whose call observed the ledger at or before it, including one still in flight when it was recorded. Either retires the original capture even when conversion names a replay. Unknown, refused, unproved, damaged, ambiguous or retired captures produce no candidate. Historical unnamespaced captures remain labeled `legacy-unnamespaced` with null namespace/generation facts; conversion never invents their route or endpoint. A replay written before #808 resolves to the latest earlier legacy capture of the same model and request digest. Those lines recorded only the enclosing task's contract for every call, so a legacy reduction, sampling or reflection capture is refused rather than relabeled with a stage contract it never recorded. A legacy response hash is explicitly `computed-legacy`, calculated during conversion rather than historically recorded; it cannot establish original response integrity or a new model's quality. Converted dialogue chunk boundaries are deterministic segmentation, not original streaming evidence.

The converter remaps cited blocks and sampling findings to the adapter's placeholders and refuses unknown block identities or placeholder-shaped text the model wrote itself. Sampling reasons may cite their own or overlap blocks through `{{sample-block:N}}`, indexed by the carried block lines in order; `{{ref:N}}` still maps only finding references, preserving digits in reasons. Everything the request carried except its frozen system prompt — blocks, earlier model and tool turns, other user text — is checked for echoes unless exact sample1 is proved as above. A reviewer still reads every free-text field and verifies rights, source identity, mappings and the intended contract before admission. Synthetic provider-free converter/loader tests establish regression behavior only; they do not establish real editorial quality or current remote-model performance.
