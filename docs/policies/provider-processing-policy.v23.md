# Provider Processing Policy v23

This is the human projection of [the immutable v23 JSON](provider-processing-policy.v23.json), validated by [its schema](provider-processing-policy.v23.schema.json). It directly succeeds the `ordinary-production` document [v21](provider-processing-policy.v21.md) and is written in the same schema family as developer-live [v22](provider-processing-policy.v22.md). It is selected by [active-set v15](active-policy-set.v15.json) under [ADR 0090](../adr/0090-admit-procedure-recommendation-foreign-skill-import-and-custom-analysis-dimensions.md) §4 and §5, [ADR 0002](../adr/0002-book-series-cross-project-and-house-learning-scopes.md) and [ADR 0037](../adr/0037-enforce-versioned-series-retrieval-exclusions-immediately.md); an internal active label grants no dispatch authority.

## In plain words

Everything v21 let the editor do with their own manuscripts, on their own computer, with their own model-service account, v23 still lets them do, byte for byte: the same nine rules, the same two model routes, the same redaction, ceilings, account-limit stop, web tools and local tools. Two things are new. The editor's own **custom analysis dimension** — a question written once in 知识库 › 工序与规则 and asked of every unit of the Book — may run on the model through a tenth rule. And the **review rule** may carry **Series Knowledge** when a review Run is scoped to a Series. Both are eligibility only: the rules authorize no Run, Apply, export or publication, and they do not by themselves make any request carry a template or a Series clause.

## What is newly allowed

**Custom analysis (the tenth rule).** `ordinary-production-custom-analysis` runs the analysis kind family `custom-analysis/<dimension-id>` on the Main Editorial Role with the editor's own configured binding, planned like the baseline: a newly user-initiated Task with a plan envelope and a Run Authorization, bound to the Coverage Manifest's units, one technical session per unit, the Run's ceiling when the editor sets one, and at most one model question per unit. It has no cross-unit reduction or synthesis, no web, no platform tool and no local retrieval (`localTools.targetedRetrieval.allowed: false`), and `source` names no Material Reference, Series Knowledge or editorial deliverable. Like every production rule it also names the three unpublished classes. The rule's `allowedTaskKinds` holds the single entry `custom-analysis/*`: a trailing `/*` admits every kind of that one family under the prefix, where the part after `custom-analysis/` is one non-empty segment; the entry is not a general pattern, no other rule uses one, and the launch resolver verifies the exact literal.

**The question template.** The rule names one more outbound data category, `editor-authored-question-template` (编辑自拟的单元提问模板), beside `public-or-synthetic`, and states its declaration in an `editorAuthoredQuestionTemplate` block: the template is an editor-authored per-unit question from a 工序与规则 document, at most **400** graphemes, attributed to the exact dimension version, never manuscript text and never an author's or an editor's name (`housePeopleNamesAndRolesStripped` stays `true`), and only a bounded data parameter of AI7's fixed contract framing — it never overrides that framing (`contractFramingOverrideAllowed: false`). No other rule names the category or the block.

**Series Knowledge on the review rule.** The review rule `ordinary-production-review` now states `source.seriesKnowledgeAllowed: true` and names the outbound data category `series-knowledge-excerpt` (书系知识摘录) with a `seriesKnowledgeExcerpt` declaration: Series Knowledge item revisions pinned by the Run's frozen Series scope, cut into at most **40** clauses of at most **300** graphemes each (the bounds 书系一致性 already keeps), only items of the pinned Series, honouring every Series Retrieval Exclusion frozen in the Run, naming the item revisions they rest on, and under `ordinary-production` only from ordinary-production sources. No other rule states `seriesKnowledgeAllowed: true` or names the category.

**The Series consistency kind.** The review rule's `allowedTaskKinds` gains `editorial-review/series-consistency` as its last kind, so that category runs live only on this rule, only over the Run's frozen Series scope, with the `series-knowledge-excerpt` category and the frozen Series Retrieval Exclusions. Which kinds a launch really sends stays the consuming slice's decision.

## What stays exactly as v21

The rules, role bindings, redaction, capture, ceilings, clarification, platform tools, local tools, 来源译文 term and 本社审阅偏好 declaration are v21's, except the review rule's additions above; for every term not named above, [the v21 projection](provider-processing-policy.v21.md) is the description.

## What stays refused

The default is deny. Everything v21 refused stays refused, except 书系一致性 on the review rule as stated above: Series Knowledge in any other rule, 出版政策与风险, any web tool outside the three research rules, hosted and CI execution, and every other task kind. A custom analysis dimension never sends tools, never mutates the Book and never widens the Run source scope.

## How a launch uses it

The launch resolver verifies this document byte for byte against the active-set pin and reads every rule whole: the custom analysis rule must state its terms and its declaration exactly; the review rule must list the Series category and kind and state the flag and the declaration exactly; and any other rule that names either category, states `seriesKnowledgeAllowed: true` or lists a `/*` kind is a policy this launch cannot read, which denies processing. Provider eligibility is not Run Authorization, Apply, export, learning, publication or credential configuration; the editor's own model-service binding still decides the route.
