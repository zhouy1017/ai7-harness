# Govern learning eligibility with versioned Policy Documents

AI7 records Learning Eligibility Policy as a human-reviewable, machine-validatable, immutable-versioned Policy Document rather than hidden prompts, code-only defaults, or model weights. [ADR 0094](0094-unified-multi-provider-runtime-global-learning-agent-and-word-track-changes-intake.md) replaces recommendation-only admission and mandatory manual activation for every derived preference with disclosed opt-out local learning and supported-pattern automatic activation inside its bounded material/scope envelope. Exclusions, narrower choices, supervision, lineage and reversible remediation govern curation and later use. Wider material/egress authority, Policy Document semantics or weakened safeguards still require an authorized policy revision; a derived preference cannot enlarge that authority. Existing immutable policy bytes remain in force until a reviewed successor is selected.
