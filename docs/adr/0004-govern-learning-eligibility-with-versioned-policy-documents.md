# Govern learning eligibility with versioned Policy Documents

AI7 records Learning Eligibility Policy as a human-reviewable, machine-validatable, immutable-versioned Policy Document rather than hidden prompts, code-only defaults, or model weights. It begins recommendation-only, may make high-confidence decisions only inside user-approved material and scope boundaries, and lets post-run AI agents author evidence-linked revisions; only evaluated non-expansive calibration inside a user-approved envelope may activate automatically, while new rules, wider scopes, semantic changes, weakened safeguards, or other authority changes require explicit user activation. This preserves adaptive workload reduction without allowing an agent or policy to silently enlarge its own authority, and every decision and activation remains auditable and reversible.
