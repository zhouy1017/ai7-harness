### The principle and why a broad amendment is needed

The canonical [Unpublished Editorial Material and Public Release Permission](../domain/editorial/CONTEXT.md) definitions already separate internal editorial processing from public disclosure. The [Owner's #1071 decision 2](https://github.com/zhouy1017/ai7-harness/issues/1071#issuecomment-6379596688) states the principle: privacy restricts public release, not transmission to the editor's own configured model provider. The [controlling correction](https://github.com/zhouy1017/ai7-harness/issues/1071#issuecomment-6382289920) keeps accepted ADR 0079 §4.4 until an Owner-accepted amendment names its affected clauses; the superseded [Commander application](https://github.com/zhouy1017/ai7-harness/issues/1071#issuecomment-6381981177) is not a current permission. The principle accepts nothing by implication.

Under ADR 0079 §4.4 the manuscript's text and the author's information may reach a Model Provider and its tools, but 责编 and 相关人 names and roles, 备注 and internal notes are stripped. Every allow rule of the selected ordinary-production [Provider Processing v27](../policies/provider-processing-policy.v27.json) keeps both redaction booleans true. The existing known-name filter (`preferenceNames` and `withoutNames` in `src/service/house-review-preference.ts`, reused by `EditorialStore.globalAgentRedact`) replaces names it already knows; it cannot show that arbitrary original text — a manuscript sentence, a decision reason, a saved instruction, a question — contains no colleague's name, house role or note wording. Original words are therefore either altered or held. ADR 0100 and ADR 0101 each resolved one bounded use and kept the strip everywhere else; ADR 0102 and ADR 0103 proposed further bounded uses. The Owner asked instead for one proposal covering the original fields of every independently authorized ordinary-production operation, reaching the existing model and the already-authorized search and fetch recipients. That recipient choice deliberately differs from ADR 0101's accepted provider-only rule and so names its clauses explicitly.

[ADR 0080 §5.3](0080-assign-a-provider-route-and-model-to-every-task-that-transmits.md#53-the-owners-decisions-of-2026-09-11-second-session--the-platform-tools-the-search-service-the-bounds) Question 2 already records the Owner's judgment that a search query is neither the full text nor publication. Its closing premise — the strip applies to everything the model reads, so no query can carry what the model never saw — depended on the strip and is replaced for this predicate (§4 row 8).

## 1. Proposed decision: ordinary-production original-field processing

After (a) the Owner's acceptance of this exact text, (b) separately authorized alignment of §4 and §5, (c) a separately reviewed and selected coherent immutable policy successor and (d) faithful typed consumers and final gates ([§6](#6-policy-and-runtime-compatibility-and-the-activation-sequence)), names, house-person roles and deliberately supplied private-note wording that occur in original text need not be removed or refused **solely because they occur**, when every one of the following holds. In this record and in every proposed clause text, “the original-field processing of ADR 0099 §1” means exactly this complete conjunction — never all scopes, all data or all configured providers. It is a descriptive conjunction, not a new runtime API, launch flag, approval step or authorization object.

1. **Operation.** The actual operation runs in the launch-selected `ordinary-production` scope with its independently authorized business purpose, source read and execution origin, and has its real identity: its Task, Plan, Run and stage where it has them; otherwise its existing explicit dialogue attempt (Book or Book-less `ask`, `continue` or `regenerate`), its foreground Fast Interaction action or the connection-test action. No Task or Run is invented for a dialogue or a foreground action. An authorized background operation — dispatched by an active matching enrollment within its mandatory ceiling, envelope and idle conditions — qualifies only under its own origin. This proposal originates no operation and removes no enrollment, envelope or idle requirement; installing, importing, enabling or configuring something authorizes nothing.
2. **Field.** Only the exact original words inside an already-admitted typed field, or inside an already-admitted bounded projection of those words, qualify. That field and its source and version, purpose, outbound category, complete package and actual recipe must already be permitted independently of this record. Existing bounds, whole-example fidelity, attribution, framing, citation and source exclusions remain. This adds no field, passes no whole record, enlarges no source read and never turns omitted data into an empty field.
3. **Model recipient.** The receiving model is the provider, route, model, protocol and credential reference and generation bound to that actual attempt or stage under its already-authorized Model Role. “Existing models” means those actual bindings, not every configured service, role or fallback chain. No role is reassigned and no capability is proved. The Credential Broker is unchanged, and secret values never become model-visible, disclosed or logged. ADR 0100's Main Editorial Role authored-typos branch and ADR 0101's separately authorized consumer roles stay as accepted; the unbound Difficult Escalation and Frontier Model Roles gain nothing.
4. **Search and fetch recipients.** Where that same operation is independently authorized to search, its already-permitted model-produced query (outbound category `web-search-query`) may go through its selected, policy-admitted search backend, and its existing citation-bound public-source fetch (`public-source-fetch`) may retrieve the actual admitted URL the model cited or the search returned. Qualifying wording may remain in exactly those two outbound fields, including wording the model derives from a qualifying field or a complete retained fragment. For this conjunction only, this expressly replaces ADR 0101 §4's categorical provider-only rule (§5). It admits no raw bundle as a new search field; no header, referrer, request body or upload body carrying it; no manufactured or unbound URL; no arbitrary host, backend or credential; and no other model, tool, subagent, stage or recipient. The search backend's own processing of a query is the processing of that selected recipient, not a new Main-role fallback.
5. **Recipe and rule.** Both the actual recipe and the selected rule must authorize each recipient and each field. A rule-level web allowance does not switch on web for a no-web recipe: tools-free stays tools-free and local tools stay local; `editorial-review/publication-risk` and `readers-report` stay without web; curation gains no search; the four local tools proposed for retained Global use gain no platform tool. The query's outbound category, existing destination and citation checks, query and request caps, response-size bounds, circuit breakers, the Run Budget Ceiling or other applicable finite budget, account-limit stopping and unknown-outcome behavior stay.
6. **Currentness at every send.** Their existing owners recheck current eligibility and the exact prepared source, control, policy and binding pins after any capacity wait and immediately before each physical model, search or fetch send, and again before publishing a new result where the consumer's contract requires it. A stale or changed pin refuses the affected new send without silent refresh, fallback or resend. Truthful sent outcome, cost, checkpoints and history are never erased; a possibly sent request is never resent automatically; a frozen version A is never retargeted to a current B. The existing source and control owners carry these checks; no universal content classifier or generic taint framework is introduced or needed.

**A content qualification only.** The manuscript's text and the author's information keep their existing permission, the house name stays unhidden, and already-permitted non-secret metadata such as frozen Book metadata and synopsis stays under its own contract; this record adds no blanket metadata ban. It admits no structured personnel, role or attribution object, no imported author or reviewer label not already admitted, no ambient metadata, credential or secret value, and no Editor Note or internal-note object merely because the same words may stand in a typed field. The model receives only the actual closed projection, never a whole memory, source, annotation, decision or Book record.

**The editor's supplied words versus the Editor Note object.** An Editor Note, internal-note object or Personal Highlight stays outside every model and Task source scope. Deliberately typing or pasting the same words into an authorized question, instruction or decision-feedback field does not authorize reading the note or its anchored manuscript. Explicit conversion to a distinct Annotation follows its existing command and any later reader's own admission; no automatic copy, note picker, conversion or learning grant follows. A native Proposal Decision reason, a Word Tracked Edit Sample and a Manual Edit Journal observation remain distinct typed source families ([ADR 0101 §2](https://github.com/zhouy1017/ai7-harness/blob/a32462d98d1cf578eea00226dc4add22799092b3/docs/adr/0101-admit-scoped-full-fidelity-learned-examples.md#2-three-closed-source-families)); no proposal, decision or reason is fabricated for the latter two, and a reason is told from a note by its record type and proven producer, never by a semantic classifier.

**Processing is not release.** Sending a bounded query to an admitted search service is processing, not public posting; publicly uploading manuscript text, publishing a response, sharing a corpus or exporting a file is not a query exception. ADR 0079 §4.1's prohibition on publishing the manuscript's text verbatim to a public-access network, exact Public Release Permission, the Local-only Export Boundary, local export preparation, approval and receipt, and the single-use Apply remain. Existing disclosure — the plan's 发送 line (V2-UX-EGR-005) and the dialogue and action disclosures — continues to name what is sent and to whom; no privacy checkbox, per-request approval or second confirmation is added. The repository, worktree, CI, log, artifact, corpus, fixture and distribution prohibitions and their exact Public SampleBook exceptions stay under [AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md#protected-material). `developer-live`, development CI and the Gate, and `fixture-recording` acquire no ordinary-production authority: development transmits only admitted Public SampleBooks, including permitted tester edits of `sample1`, and another manuscript pasted into it stays protected. No material, dependency or credential admission or acquisition, no provider call and no per-example or per-fixture approval ceremony follows.

## 2. Retained fragments, mixed contexts and history

**Retained fragments.** The conjunction governs every later physical send, not only the first payload. A retained fragment qualifies only as an actual complete fragment the existing Harness Session Ledger owns for the bound attempt, under the consumer's exact permitted continuation relation, with its full provenance — the union of every source that influenced it — and every applicable source and control restriction still satisfied. It creates no third transcript. It never recovers redacted originals from an older answer, reconstructs an old missing pin, relabels an old redacted attempt, retargets A to B or carries words from one Task to another.

**Mixed contexts.** A context in which every relevant field and fragment satisfies the same conjunction may use its existing query or cited-fetch path without a word-by-word classifier deciding which words came from an instruction and which from a raw example. The proof is closed field assembly, complete source provenance and currentness, and the exact recipient gates. If a context also contains a separately restricted source, a fragment of unknown provenance or a predecessor binding that does not permit the new processing, either that remaining restriction is satisfied or the affected new processing is held. No lossy fragment elision, hidden pre-pass, replacement session or disabled required search may stand in for the complete intended consumer, and a later plain path runs only where its own contract truthfully exists, never as a silent fallback from a stale prepared raw request. This is not universal name detection, a prompt prohibition, a sampled non-leak observation or a generic taint engine.

**Preparation and history.** Old policy-bound Plans, attempts and completed reports keep their original bytes, rules and outcomes. A fresh explicit preparation under a selected faithful successor may read the actual authorized originals; it cannot upgrade an old attempt or invent stripped words. An unavailable, corrupt or stale source is never a zero-eligible fallback; independently authorized plain behavior continues only where it truthfully exists.

## 8. Consequences, rejected alternatives and the future Owner question

**Consequences.** If accepted and delivered, an ordinary-production operation keeps the editor's real words across every independently admitted original field, so analyses, examples, instructions and questions are not degraded or held merely for a colleague's name or a note's wording. The bound model, and where the same operation already searches, its selected search service and the cited public page's host, may receive such wording. No provider term, confidentiality guarantee or new recipient is inferred. Structured personnel and note objects never become sources, and public release keeps its separate authority. The cost is coordinated later policy, consumer, fixture and disclosure work; it cannot be delivered as a filter toggle. If this text is not accepted, today's restrictions and the accepted ADR 0100 and ADR 0101 predicates remain the behavior.

**Rejected alternatives.**

- *Accept the original narrow ADR 0099*: declined by the Owner (「要求另拟更广的生产范围」).
- *Model-only recipients, keeping ADR 0101's provider-only rule everywhere*: not selected; the Owner chose the existing model plus the already-authorized search and fetch recipients.
- *A per-feature privacy registry or separate privacy acceptances for ADR 0102 and ADR 0103*: duplicates one decision and invites drift between overlapping predicates.
- *A universal name, role or note classifier, or a generic taint engine*: cannot prove absence in arbitrary text and would be a hidden rule.
- *Flipping the shared redaction booleans, or a policy overlay*: lifts the strip for unrelated payloads and breaks immutable policy history.
- *A per-example or per-request clearance checkbox*: reintroduces the per-item approval ADR 0094 §3 removed.
- *Converting research recipes to tools-free, or dropping required search, to avoid the recipient question*: falsifies the consumer the editor asked for.
- *Treating a search query as publication*: contradicts ADR 0080 §5.3 Question 2, which the Owner already decided.
- *Admitting Note objects, personnel records or imported attribution with the words*: outside the Owner's direction and unnecessary for original-field fidelity.
