# 0095 · Make procedures the central object, govern concurrent execution, and use DSH as the complex-task harness

On 2026-10-23 the Owner read the read-only assessment of the integrated product at `dev@be7babaf` and decided the direction recorded here. This is design, not implementation or evidence of a working provider protocol; [PROGRESS](../../PROGRESS.md) owns delivered behavior and the [development plan](../development/development-plan.md) owns order. Immutable Policy Documents keep their bytes until the successor named in §6 activates.

## The value and service mainline this record serves

AI7 is the local workbench of a literary publishing house's editor. The manuscript is the center; an agent harness takes on the complex reading, checking and drafting labour; every change to the manuscript and every 发稿 is the editor's decision and leaves an exact record. The target is delivery quality comparable to an editor's with materially less work.

The service mainline: (1) a manuscript arrives with its revisions and comments, its structure recognized and the original kept whole; (2) the editor starts a procedure (工序) with one action — a pre-installed one or the house's own — and the editor's instructions and preferences enter it; (3) the harness performs the complex processing: a whole-book read with a ledger, parallel chunked review, claim extraction and web verification, cross-chapter consistency, synthesis, tools to look back into the manuscript and materials, and non-urgent parts in idle time; (4) results return to the manuscript as exactly anchored cards with their evidence, and reports are derived views; (5) the editor reviews card by card, with batch for mechanical cases, everything reversible, the Manuscript Revision the only truth; (6) delivery through milestones, 发稿版本, Production Documents, DOCX export carrying marks as Word revisions and comments, and the 图书交付包; (7) learning and reuse: decisions, rewrites and Word revision samples become house memory, the learning agent asks why when it needs to, procedures are captured, improved and reused across Books.

The Owner also supplied a motivation example — a 22万字 review manuscript, a 255-character editor instruction, a cowork-produced monolithic report and a seven-stage procedure manual — **to align the way of working, not the deliverable**: the example's method is intent plus procedure, a whole-book ledger held by one main thread, parallel chunk workers under strict templates, an evidence chain of verbatim quotes and fetched sources, a deterministic merge gate and a second round. AI7's cards on the manuscript are the better output; the method is what §§1–5 adopt.

## 1. Every task is one run of a procedure

A **Procedure Definition** (`工序定义`, schema `ai7.procedure-definition/2`) is an AI7-owned, versioned, digest-pinned document that states a role and the editor's task instruction, an ordered list of stages, and the output kinds a run produces. Each stage names its **stage type** from a closed, product-built registry — 通读记账 (read-through ledger), 分块审读 (chunked review under a unit contract), 确定性检查 (deterministic check, which sends nothing), 断言抽取 (claim extraction), 联网取证 (web verification), 跨单元归并与跨章一致性 (cross-unit reduction and cross-chapter consistency), 综合 (synthesis), 起草 (drafting) and 派生报告 (derived report) — and its inputs (manuscript scope or a prior stage's artifact), its tool whitelist, its parallelism, its output template, its exemption rules (for example 「作者文风短促、省主语、文白夹杂不算病句」), its budget share and whether it may use the web. Output kinds are 修改建议 cards, 批注 cards, records and documents.

**All tasks are runs of procedures.** The existing baseline analysis, the nine review categories, AI7 初评 and 审稿意见, and writing become **pre-installed procedures v1**, each one definition that re-expresses the current contracts and stages without changing behavior; a pre-installed procedure passes the same Journeys its kind passes today before any new stage type is added. The house and the editor may rewrite a pre-installed procedure into a new version, create one, capture one from a completed run, version it, and reuse it across Books; 知识库 › 工序与规则 is the **procedure library**, the house's asset, and every run pins the exact procedure version it ran.

The editor's task instruction travels under a new bounded outbound data category, **编辑任务说明** (§6): a bounded number of graphemes, attributed to the procedure version, never a house person's name, and never an override of AI7's contract framing — the rule the Custom Analysis Dimension template already keeps. The Custom Analysis Dimension of ADR 0090 §4 is now one instruction form of a 分块审读 stage. Every produced finding still passes the exact-anchor gate before it becomes a mark; writing the manuscript still crosses the single-use AI7 Apply.

A Procedure Definition may be projected into a native DSH Skill or Agent Preset behind the ADR 0045 sidecars so that the harness composes it; the projection carries no authority of its own, and the execution of foreign Skills remains deferred (F-15). ADR 0087's Captured Procedure (`ai7.captured-procedure/1`) remains readable as the first, review-only form of this object.

## 2. An execution governor and real throughput

AI7 replaces the single foreground execution place with a **two-layer governor**: a global cap on model requests in flight (default 4, configurable) and a per-Run unit parallelism (default 3, configurable), with several foreground Runs across Books at once as ADR 0021 already requires. Each unit of a Run executes through its own harness composition and adapter instance; a unit's one technical session rule is unchanged, so parallel units are parallel sessions, never an accumulating one.

Every model request carries a deadline and an abort signal. A request that times out or is aborted **after it was sent** settles as outcome-unknown, never as an interrupted, resendable attempt; nothing that may have been billed is resent automatically (ADR 0067). A bare HTTP 429 on a route whose declaration marks rate limits as retryable is a rate limit: AI7 waits for `Retry-After` or a bounded backoff and retries within the Run's adaptation bound; an account limit — a 402, or a body naming quota or balance — still ends the attempt with no fallback. The OpenCode Go declaration's `account-limit-terminal` reading of a bare 429 is re-evidenced under ADR 0073 §2 before it changes.

An outcome-unknown unit is **isolated to that unit**: the Run completes with that unit as a gap, its revision and outcome are recorded, and the editor may later request 「重新发送该单元」 as a newly authorized attempt whose preview discloses the possible double billing. This is the "explicitly safe non-repeating path" CONT-011 leaves open; it is not an automatic retry. The Run-level stop that today holds a baseline Run in 结果待确认 until the editor cancels is removed.

A Review Run approval that finds every place taken **queues** (`等待运行名额`) like every other kind instead of being refused. The liveness signal shows every unit in flight with the instant its attempt started; the stale rule of ADR 0071 §3 applies per unit. The Run Budget Ceiling is evaluated with in-flight reservations: a dispatch is admitted only when the ceiling can cover every request already in flight plus the new one. The background share of the governor is configuration, not a policy byte.

## 3. An idle-time background lane

A task may be started as 「立即」 or 「空闲时处理」, and a house may enroll kinds that need no immediate result — analysis sync, Material Index and embedding builds, learning curation passes, factual re-checks, custom analysis, and the deferred stages of a procedure — in a **Background Processing Enrollment** that generalizes ADR 0048's Background Analysis Enrollment: disclosed, revocable, with a mandatory budget ceiling and the same exact per-dispatch Plan, binding and authorization. Idle means 30 seconds without an edit, checked every five seconds, as the background analysis dispatcher already does. A background Run yields its foreground places when the editor returns to the keyboard; its requests in flight are not aborted. Background work still originates only from an explicit queueing action or an enrollment, never from opening a page or importing a file (ADR 0094 §6).

## 4. DSH is the harness for complex tasks

The pinned DeepSeek Harness drives multi-step, tool-using model work inside AI7's authority envelope:

- **Tools are declared AI7 capabilities.** A policy rule names the read-only local tools a stage may register — `retrieve` and `exact_fetch` as today, plus `read_blocks` (neighbouring paragraphs inside the Run Source Scope), `baseline_facts` (entities, settings and events of the Book's latest baseline), `guideline_clause`, `term_table` and `search_manuscript` — and the prompt contract instructs the model to use them. Review prompts no longer forbid tool use.
- **Parallel tool calls** are allowed for tools that declare themselves concurrency-safe; ADR 0080 §7.4's "`maxParallelToolCalls` stays 1" and "executes tool calls one at a time" are replaced. The circuit breakers, the one-search-per-request-per-query rule and the Run Budget Ceiling stay.
- **The composition descriptor is versioned.** Composition v1 stays frozen for plans that pinned it; new plans pin v2. A stopped Run resumes only under the composition its binding pinned, or is redone under a new plan.
- **Sub-agents run inside the parent attempt.** AI7 may install the DSH sub-agent seam after reviewing its package and license. A sub-agent is declared in the Plan Envelope, inherits the Run's scope, binding, policy and ceiling, counts toward the transmission bound, passes the same final Egress Gate, and creates no AI7 Task, Run or decision record. Procedures use sub-agents for chunk fan-out and for reduction.
- **In-turn transient recovery** uses the harness's request-error waterfall for failures that provably happened before a request was sent; everything after a send follows §2.
- **What stays excluded**: shell, process runner, roaming filesystem, arbitrary network and the DSH code runtime; the dormant mount's assertions are updated with each admitted package. Composition-level choices — which plugins, tools and presets compose a procedure — remain AI7's and are expressed in editorial terms.

## 5. Review contract v2, evidence on the card, and the derived report

- **Numbered blocks.** The unit message numbers every block it prints; the model answers with that number. **Per-finding tolerance**: a finding that fails the contract is dropped alone with a reason code, never together with its unit. One bounded **contract retry** is a declared Plan Adaptation counted in the transmission bound. **Excluded findings are visible** to the editor with their text and reason; they never become marks. A prompt contract's identity is its version id mapped to immutable bytes; fixtures and replay keep their digests, and reuse compatibility is defined by contract version.
- **Review gains cross-unit stages.** A reduction and a 跨章一致性 stage — first appearances of names, years, ages, places and institutions; timeline arithmetic; whether an institution or place existed in the narrated year — make 体例与格式 and 情节逻辑与前后一致 model stages fed by unit findings and baseline leads.
- **A deterministic checker is a stage type** that transmits nothing: national punctuation and numeral usage rules, the standard character table and variant-word list, a house's own sensitive-word list, and the Book's term, name and place table.
- **Evidence lives on the card.** For a factual finding the card shows the source link, the verbatim snippet, its 来源译文 and the note; 来源与证据 archives the same evidence; a report export attaches it per finding. A **claim ledger** deduplicates and groups claims across units, allows more than one search per claim under a policy key, prefers several independent sources, records 无法确证 → 待核 for a second round, and excludes a source with lineage instead of deleting it.
- **The 审阅报告 is a derived view** — overview, 必须处理, chapter-by-category statistics, duplicates merged, each finding with its quote, suggested text, reason and sources — exported in the manuscript's formats with tables and links. It is never the primary deliverable; the cards are.
- **出版政策与风险 executes without web**, in production, as risk points only with the 未联网核查 disclosure; its row no longer requires a web-search capability.

## 6. One production policy, a runtime model table, and two new outbound categories

One successor Provider Processing policy (v26) with one active policy set (v18) carries every policy byte §§1–5 need: the governor keys and background share, the tool and parallel-call keys, the contract-retry adaptation, the review reduction/synthesis allowance, the publication-risk kind, the claim-ledger search key, and the two new outbound categories **编辑任务说明** and **本社审阅规范条款** (at most 40 clauses of at most 300 graphemes, attributed to the imported document version, never a person's name). After v26, a model, route or endpoint inside an already declared protocol family with capability evidence is a **reviewed configuration record** (a provider document plus the Model Service setting), not a new immutable policy version; the exact per-Run binding is still frozen into the Execution Binding, launch authority still selects the single operational scope, the Egress Gate still decides every byte, and the credential broker is unchanged. A new protocol family, outbound category, tool or scope still needs a policy successor. The duplicated TypeScript mirror of policy constants retires: code reads the policy and the configuration records. Developer-live keeps its public-or-synthetic restriction.

## 7. The learning agent may ask why

Within ADR 0094 §3's disclosed learning envelope, the learning agent may **ask the editor for the reason behind a decision** when a reason would make the learning useful — a rejection, a 修改后接受 whose rewrite departs from the suggestion, the same kind of change made repeatedly, or a decision that conflicts with an existing preference. The ask is a short card of options plus an optional free text, nothing preselected, never blocking the review, and the editor sets how often it may appear, down to off. Answers enter learning admission and memory with lineage like every other decision signal. This extends the existing 就地反馈轻问 and the 拒绝 reason card; it adds no new source or egress authority.
