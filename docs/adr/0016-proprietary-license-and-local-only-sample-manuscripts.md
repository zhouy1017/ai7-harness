# Keep AI7 proprietary and sample manuscripts local-only

AI7 is proprietary and its rights rest solely with the repository owner. The repository carries an explicit all-rights-reserved `LICENSE` rather than relying on default copyright, because an unstated license reads as unresolved and had been blocking source reuse. No open-source grant is made: AI7 is a commercial product for mainland Chinese publishing houses, it holds unpublished editorial material, and it is built partly on a private predecessor. A proprietary license does not discharge inbound obligations — DeepSeek Harness is MIT and its history also carries BSD-3-Clause material, a BSD-licensed native component, and vendored payload obligations, some of which may be identity- or distribution-scoped and must be verified rather than assumed to extend to an AI7-branded distribution. Any AI7 build therefore ships a maintained third-party notices file.

The owner is the sole rights-holder of the private predecessor `ai7-reborn-ai` and authorizes reuse of its code, documentation, tests, and fixtures for AI7 under the existing provenance rules. This removes the last authority blocker on source copying; the per-asset provenance ledger, sanitization review, and provider-terms checks all still apply.

Rights in the sample manuscripts are separate from rights in the code, and are narrower. The existing sample Books are real manuscripts, authorized for AI7 project use on the condition that they remain private and local. They are never committed to any repository in history or working tree, never enter hosted CI, build artifacts, distributable fixtures, or the shipped product, never become mock-provider evidence or a corpus, and carry no redistribution right. Outbound transmission is a separate decision this authorization does not grant: under the accepted plan-envelope boundary, reading material within a Run Source Scope never by itself permits sending it outward, so a private sample Book stays on the owner's device unless a distinct decision says otherwise. Authorization to use is not authorization to publish, share, or transmit.
