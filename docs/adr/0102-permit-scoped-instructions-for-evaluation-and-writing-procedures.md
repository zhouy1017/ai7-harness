## 1. Proposed decision and exact scope

After Owner exact-text acceptance, separately assigned dependent alignment, separately reviewed and selected immutable evaluation/writing policy and fixture successors, and a faithful accepted typed F26 consumer, **only the following conjunction** would exempt the exact saved instruction from removal or refusal solely because it contains a person's name, a house-person role or deliberately supplied private-note wording. Every reference to **ADR 0102 §1** means this entire section, including every source, authority, recipient, composition and currentness condition; no individual clause is a freestanding permission.

1. **Purpose and origin.** Ordinary-production, an explicitly initiated foreground Task of exactly `evaluation`, `evaluation-rewrite`, `readers-report` or `writing`, using its actually supported procedure recipe and the actual accepted role, Execution Binding, Task, Plan, Run and stage authority. No role is assigned by this exception. Saving, creating, capturing or enabling a procedure, import, opening a page, passive defaults, Global/Book dialogue, arbitrary review/F04, curation, background/enrollment or Deferred Start does not qualify. Existing authorized safe same-Run continuation/resume keeps its exact limits; queuing does not create a new origin, and no terminal revival or resend permission follows.

2. **Exact field and fidelity.** The sole exempt source is the real saved immutable Procedure Version's original exact `editorInstruction`, at most **1,200 graphemes**, attributed and pinned to its procedure identity, version and document digest and to the exact Task/Plan/Run/stage. It remains the **`editor-task-instruction` / 编辑任务说明** category and stays subordinate to AI7's contract framing, output contracts and authority. It cannot expand a readable scope, tool set, role, budget or effect. Names, house-person roles and deliberately supplied note wording alone do not require stripping or refusing this field within the conjunction. An invalid, missing, corrupt or oversized source is refused or held whole by the actual consumer; no trimming, normalization, redaction, truncation or substituted text may be relabeled as the exact original. Explicit procedure editing/capture uses its real owner and creates or retains a real version; it cannot reconstruct removed words or borrow a historical pin.

3. **Other data retains its own contract.** Procedure title, description and origin/provenance; evaluation entries, scores, comments and finalized records; writing audience, channel and requirements; manuscript, materials, exemplars, guidelines, produced documents and all their metadata keep their separate existing source and redaction restrictions. There is no automatic copying of those fields into `editorInstruction`, no ambient Book/library/people/path/credential/secret join, and no new source category. Deliberately supplied words are editor input, not an **Editor Note object**: they confer no Note-object/picker/anchor or anchored-manuscript read, automatic Note copying or learning admission. A request to retrieve the former Note is a separate source request and gains no authority here. Existing legitimate manuscript/material reads remain governed by their own exact grants; the instruction is no shortcut to them.

4. **I-B recipient consequence.** The model provider bound to this exact Task/Run/stage may receive the saved field. Instruction-derived words may additionally reach **only this same Task's already-authorized search-query field and actual citation-bound fetch URL**, to its selected existing search backend and the destination admitted by the existing cited-URL gate. This is a proposed field privacy exception, not operation authority. The actual recipe still decides whether the operation exists: **readers-report remains no-web**, and evaluation-market/writing retain their required research. A broadly web-enabled policy rule alone proves neither operation admission nor recipient authority. Existing argument, source/category, cited-URL, byte, time, budget, credential and final-gate controls all remain. No new search backend, host wildcard, method, request body, header, referrer, upload/public post, external tool, subagent, alternate model or unrelated Task/session is admitted. The URL exception is the actual admitted citation-bound URL, not arbitrary URLs attached as context or permission to bypass destination validation. The bound provider and those two named existing research fields are the entire proposed recipient consequence; it supplies no secondary route for other source text.

5. **Mixed contexts remain conjunctive.** An instruction-bearing request must satisfy every included source's independent predicate through all tool calls, adaptations, stages and retained fragments. This exception does not admit ADR 0101 learned examples, G curation guides or R retained research/Annotation/reply passages to these four Tasks, and does not let their words reach a secondary recipient under their separate prospective provider-only contracts. If a source is separately admitted later, actual all-predicate proof must establish that its recipient restrictions survive the complete composition. Without that proof, **hold the new mixed instruction-bearing use** while preserving independently authorized existing behavior and required search. Never turn a research recipe into tools0, drop a required operation or claim the original recipe complete merely to avoid the privacy boundary.

6. **No invented proof.** A model's assertion does not establish that wording is instruction-derived. A known-literal scan, universal-name classifier assumption, prompt warning, model assurance, no-tools declaration, sampled non-leak, fake sanitized summary or hidden split/prepass/extra session does not prove source isolation or field provenance. Future work must establish the actual typed producer, source/composition and binding boundary; uncertain mixed-origin forwarding remains held. This documentation child defines no generic taint architecture, classifier, API or registry, and a policy allow field is not a substitute for a real consumer.

7. **Currentness, sends and history.** Validate actual source/version/text, procedure/document and relevant composition pins, current source controls, policy, recipient, binding and Task/Plan/Run/stage authority during preparation and, **after capacity, immediately before each physical send**, including every tool continuation. The actual disclosed instruction and the submitted instruction must be the same exact saved text with the same attribution. Apply the accepted consumer's separate prepublication guards before publishing a new result, finding, proposal or suggestion. Source, control, policy, recipient or binding drift refuses the stale send/publication without repinning, current-version substitution, fallback, extra model or resend. If A was prepared/authorized and B is later saved, exact A remains A under ordinary freshness and same-Run resume rules; B never silently replaces it. Preserve truthful sent/no-send/unknown outcomes, costs, old payloads, committed results and history. There is no reconstruction of original text from redacted history, fabricated historical Procedure pin, old-byte reinterpretation or implicit history migration; local inspection is not new-use authority. Existing stop, account-limit, cancellation, interruption, safe-retry and never-resend-after-possible-send limits remain.

8. **Ordinary disclosure and independent boundaries.** Normal preparation/processing/sending disclosure names the exact instruction text, version/source scope and actual recipients, including the consequence that deliberately supplied names/private wording may reach the bound model provider and, where the recipe already authorizes it, the existing research service or cited fetch host. No provider secrecy/confidentiality guarantee, new privacy confirmation, checkbox, per-call prompt or NER attestation follows. Public Release Permission and the prohibition on publishing manuscript text verbatim to a public-access network, export preparation/approval/receipt, single-use Apply and manuscript control, budgets and external-effect boundaries remain independent. Repository/worktree/CI/development material restrictions stay unchanged; development admits only its authorized Public SampleBooks and CI/Gate remain provider-free ([AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md#protected-material), [project constraints](../agents/project-constraints.md#ownership-provenance-and-protected-material)). No current finite function/route or required research is disabled by this proposal, and no dependency, credential, paid fallback, public posting, release or `main` promotion is authorized.

I-B deliberately permits instruction-derived wording at an already-authorized research recipient. ADR 0100/0101's provider-only proposals do not gain that consequence, and this proposal does not make all four-family recipes tool-free. The tradeoff is real: the named people/private wording the editor deliberately saves may reach those existing recipients. Retaining today's strip/hold remains current behavior unless the exact design is accepted and its separate delivery conditions are met.
