# Separate textual fidelity from factual verification

AI7 treats an exact manuscript/source revision as the Textual Source of Record for what the document says, while treating its factual and semantic assertions as claims that may be wrong. Verification follows a versioned Factual Verification Policy Document with domain-appropriate evidence, explicit roles and provenance; Foundation Model knowledge can initiate research but cannot verify a fact by itself, and insufficient or conflicting evidence remains visible rather than being silently resolved. Reference Integrity, Claim Support, and Factual Verification are recorded independently, and any fix remains an evidence-linked, exact-revision Correction Proposal or editor/author query until the applicable mutation decision is made.
